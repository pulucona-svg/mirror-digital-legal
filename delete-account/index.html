Account Deletion & Data Retention Guide

Applicable Platform: Mirror Digital Mobile Application
Document Version: 1.0
Effective Date: October 1, 2026
Grace Period Window: 30 Calendar Days
Informational Guidance Only:

This page provides transparent information regarding the account deletion lifecycle in Mirror Digital. Visiting or reading this web page does not automatically delete your account or submit a request. Account deletion must be initiated directly by the authenticated user within the Mirror Digital mobile application.

1. Deletion Overview

At Mirror Digital, we recognize that users may choose to close their accounts. To protect students and educators against accidental data loss while honoring privacy rights, our platform uses a multi-stage account deletion architecture.

Rather than immediately purging your learning history upon a single click, our backend enforces a structured lifecycle:

Stage 1: Deletion Initiated In-App

The authenticated user submits an account deletion request through the mobile application's Profile menu. The account status is flagged as pending deletion, active credentials and sessions are signed out, and the user is returned to the login screen.

Stage 2: 30-Day Grace Period

A mandatory 30-calendar-day grace period begins. Your account profile is hidden from active public interaction, but core data is preserved temporarily to allow for recovery in case of unauthorized access or reconsideration.

Stage 3: Account Restoration Window

Before the 30-day deadline expires, users can cancel the pending deletion request simply by logging into the application and tapping the Restore Account button on the restoration countdown screen.

Stage 4: Permanent Deletion & Anonymization

Once the 30 days expire without a restoration request, automated Firebase Cloud Functions execute the permanent deletion lifecycle. Authentication records and private profile data are permanently removed, while contributed educational materials are preserved in anonymized form.

Stage 5: General Retention & Archival Considerations

Shared academic repository materials, community study contributions, and non-personal educational records may be retained in general terms to maintain curriculum access and academic continuity for other users.

2. In-App Deletion Steps

To initiate account deletion, follow these verified steps within the Mirror Digital Flutter mobile application:

  1. Open Mirror Digital: Launch the application on your mobile device and ensure you are signed in.
  2. Navigate to Profile: Select the Profile tab from the application navigation.
  3. Open More Options: In the top-right corner of the Profile screen app bar, tap the More options overflow menu button (represented by three vertical dots: ⋮).
  4. Select Delete Account: From the dropdown menu, tap Delete Account (displayed with a red deletion icon).
  5. Review Deletion Information: On the Delete account screen, read the "How Account Deletion Works" card detailing the 30-day grace period. (You may optionally provide feedback in the optional survey).
  6. Tap Delete Account: Scroll to the bottom of the screen and tap the red Delete Account button.
  7. Confirm Deletion: In the Confirm Account Deletion modal dialog, confirm by tapping Yes, Request Deletion.
  8. Deactivation & Sign-out: Your account enters the 30-day pending grace period immediately, your active session is signed out, and you are returned to the Login screen with an informational confirmation notification.

3. 30-Day Grace Period

The 30-day grace period provides a safety net against unintended loss of study notes, bookmarks, and account access:

  • Duration: Exactly 30 calendar days from the timestamp of the in-app deletion request.
  • Visibility: During this period, your profile is suspended and will not appear in public student directories or collaborative views.
  • Data State: Your user records remain in Cloud Firestore, marked with an active deletion pending timestamp.

4. Account Restoration Window

If you change your mind during the 30-day grace period, restoring your account is simple:

  1. Open the Mirror Digital mobile application on your device.
  2. Sign in using the same credentials (email/password or Google Sign-In) associated with the account.
  3. The application automatically detects your pending deletion status and presents the Account Restoration screen with an authoritative countdown timer showing the remaining days and hours.
  4. Tap the Restore Account button.
  5. The backend verifies your request, resets your deletion status to active, restores your saved study preferences, and redirects you to the dashboard.
Irreversible Deadline:

Once the 30-day grace period expires, account restoration is no longer possible under any circumstances. Automated backend processes permanently remove or anonymize your credentials.

5. Permanent Deletion & Data Anonymization

When the 30-day grace period concludes without a restoration request, automated Firebase Cloud Functions execute the permanent data deletion protocol:

  • Authentication Credentials: Your Firebase Authentication record is deleted, permanently removing your ability to log into the account.
  • Personal Profile Fields: Identifying profile attributes (including display names, contact information, and personal preferences) are removed or scrubbed.
  • Anonymization: In accordance with the system design, user activity records that cannot be immediately purged are stripped of direct personal identifiers so they can no longer be associated with you.

6. Educational Content & Archival Retention

Mirror Digital is an educational platform designed to support curriculum study, research materials, and academic archives.

Educational Materials & Retention Guidelines:

Account deletion removes or anonymizes your personal account credentials and personal profile data. However, public educational archive materials, shared study resources, and general academic content may be preserved to ensure uninterrupted educational access for students and researchers.

Scope of Preservation: Sourced curricula, published educational resources, and academic reference materials remain intact to maintain learning continuity, academic citations, and platform integrity across the broader learning community.

7. Third-Party & Financial Records Retention

In compliance with data protection principles and statutory accounting obligations, certain categories of records cannot be purged immediately upon account deletion:

  • Financial and Transaction Records: If you purchased optional packages or passes through Paystack, cryptographic transaction reference IDs, timestamps, and payment amounts are retained in accordance with statutory accounting and tax regulations.
  • CDN Cached Media: Media files stored or cached on ImageKit edge CDN nodes expire according to automated cache invalidation cycles and scheduled media purges.
  • Disaster Recovery Backups: Routine encrypted database backups maintained for disaster recovery and operational resilience are overwritten and rotated on standard cloud operational cycles.
