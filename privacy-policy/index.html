Mirror Digital ("we", "our", or "Platform") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information.

By creating an account, accessing, browsing or using Mirror Digital, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. ABOUT THIS PRIVACY POLICY

Mirror Digital ("we", "our", or "Platform") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information. Mirror Digital determines the purposes and means of processing and may use third-party data processors or service providers to provide specific services.

The contact email for privacy and data-related requests is:

mirrordigitalofficial@gmail.com

Depending on how you use the Platform, we may collect or process different categories of information.

We do not necessarily collect every category from every user.

Mirror Digital may process uploaded material to:

• address copyright complaints;
• maintain security; and
• operate the Platform.

Where optional paid packages or passes are purchased, transactions (including M-Pesa, Airtel Money, and card options) are securely processed through Paystack. Payment interactions take place directly via Paystack's interface, and Mirror Digital records cryptographic transaction reference IDs and payment timestamps to fulfill purchased access.

24. ACCOUNT DELETION

You may request deletion of your Mirror Digital account directly through the application profile settings or by contacting Mirror Digital.

When you request account deletion through the Platform, your account enters an authoritative 30-day grace period during which you may restore your account at any time by signing in and choosing to restore. If you do not restore your account within 30 days, permanent deletion and anonymization occur automatically.

Where account deletion is requested, you may also contact:

mirrordigitalofficial@gmail.com

Educational materials and comments you contributed may be preserved in anonymized form to maintain academic continuity for peers.

Deletion may not immediately remove every record from active or backup systems.

Some information may need to be retained for lawful reasons, including:
• financial records;
• fraud prevention;
• security;
• legal claims;
• copyright disputes;
• regulatory requirements;
• enforcement of Platform rules;
• audit records; or
• backups.

Where information is retained, we seek to limit its use to the purposes for which retention is necessary.

For a detailed step-by-step walkthrough of the mobile application's deletion controls, restoration countdown, and archive retention rules, please visit our dedicated Account Deletion Guide.
